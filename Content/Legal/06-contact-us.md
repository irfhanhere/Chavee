## What Can We Help You With?

### For Students

If you're a current or prospective Chavee user, reach out to us for:

- **Account issues** — trouble signing up, logging in, or verifying your email
- **Profile and privacy questions** — how to control what information is visible to other users
- **Reporting a problem** — inappropriate content, a suspicious gig listing, or a user violating our Community Guidelines
- **Feature requests** — ideas for what you'd like to see next on Chavee (we genuinely read these)
- **Technical bugs** — anything not working as expected on the platform or mobile app

For urgent safety concerns (harassment, threats, or content requiring immediate moderation), please use the in-app reporting feature available on posts, messages, and profiles, in addition to emailing us — this ensures your report reaches our moderation queue directly and gets prioritized appropriately.

### For Colleges and Institutions

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How quickly will I get a response?**
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours on business days. Urgent safety-related reports submitted through the in-app reporting tool are typically reviewed faster, as they go directly into our moderation queue.

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the Chavee login page. You'll receive a password reset link via email — if you don't see it within a few minutes, please check your spam folder before contacting support.

**How do I delete my Chavee account?**
Account deletion options are available in your profile settings. If you're having trouble locating this option, email us and we'll guide you through the process, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

**Is Chavee free to use?**
Yes, Chavee's core features — including social networking, gig applications, community access, and mentorship matching — are free for students. Certain premium features may be introduced over time, and any paid offerings will always be clearly labeled with transparent pricing.

**How do I verify my college on Chavee?**
During signup, you'll be prompted to enter your college details. Verification helps maintain a trusted, student-only community. If your college isn't yet recognized in our system, contact us and we'll work to get it added.

**I found a suspicious or fake gig listing. What should I do?**
Please use the "Report" option available on the gig listing itself, or email us directly with the listing details. All reported gigs are reviewed by our moderation team, and listings requiring admin verification are not made publicly visible until approved.

**Can I use Chavee if I'm not in Kerala?**
Yes. While Chavee was founded and is currently strongest in Kerala, the platform is open to students across India, and we're actively growing our presence in colleges and universities nationwide.

**Does Chavee have a mobile app?**
Chavee is accessible through any mobile browser at chavee.in, with a fully responsive experience designed for phones and tablets. A dedicated mobile app is part of our ongoing roadmap — follow our updates for the latest on availability.
